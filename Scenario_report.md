# Scenario report
### Description: The goal of this report is to identify which energy sources are optimal for different scenarios, such as for specific regions/states, financial budget, business needs (only x amount of MMWH needed), or best energy sources in specific energy sectors.

### Scenario 1 - Regional optimization
Description: Which energy sources are optimal for the top 5 most populated states <br>

### Scenario 2 - Budget-Constrained scenarios 
Description: Which energy sources are optimal under a strict financial limit starting from 50$/MWh, 100$/MWh, 150$/MWh, etc <br>

### Scenario 3 - Business or industrial case
Description: Find the optimal energy source selection for the specific consumption needs <br>

### Scenario 4 - Energy sector-specific 
Description: Find the optimal energy source for specific energy sectors like transportation, residential, or agriculture <br>
